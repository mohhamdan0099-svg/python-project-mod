About Mawid

Mawid means appointment. We built it so booking a professional in the West Bank is as simple as picking a time.

Why we built it

Booking a doctor, a tutor or a plumber often means phone calls and waiting for an answer. Mawid shows the real free times of every professional, so you book without calling, and professionals fill their day without double bookings.

Every professional is reviewed by an admin before appearing on the site. We check their city, address, phone number and proof of work.
